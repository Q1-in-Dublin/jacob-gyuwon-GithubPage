Day to day, that means building and maintaining the ETL pipelines that move regulatory and custodian data through Utmost Group's investment platform.

Pipelines & data

I build and maintain the production ETL pipelines in Python and T-SQL, automate custodian data ingestion over SFTP, and write new converters whenever a financial data format changes.

Reliability & incidents

I investigate and resolve five to ten production incidents a month, lead the post-incident reviews, and keep automated unit, integration, and performance tests running against live databases.

Stakeholders & delivery

I design and deliver the back-end APIs that move data to six custodian banks, work with them directly on requirements and data quality, and write up findings in bilingual Korean and English reports.

Reporting & tooling

I build Power BI reports for executive stakeholders and use AI code-generation tooling in production, saving the team roughly four hours a week on manual conversion work.
